# Cookie Clicker Bot

🌐 **English** · [Dansk](Home-da) · [Bahasa Melayu](Home-ms)

A Selenium bot that plays [Cookie Clicker](https://ozh.github.io/cookieclicker/) for five minutes: it clicks the big cookie, buys upgrades and reports its cookies per second (CPS). It is the day 48 project of Udemy's *100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp*.

This wiki walks through the five lecture steps. Each page has a screenshot taken by the bot itself in a headless browser inside Docker, the function that does the step, and what the step taught.
